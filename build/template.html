How to use this bank

Study loop

Pick your option(s), press Enter to check, then read all four explanation panels before moving on. The explanations are the point — the question is just the hook. Flag anything you got right by luck.

Recommended sequence

1. Work one domain at a time on Easy + Medium until you are above 85%.
2. Switch Status to Missed and re-drill until it empties.
3. Filter to Hard across all domains — this is what separates a pass from a comfortable pass.
4. Finish with Flagged the day before you sit the exam.

Flashcards

The Flashcards tab is a separate active-recall deck: one concept per card, with a one-line hook to carry into the exam. Cards you mark Need review collect in a review pile you can drill on its own. Use these to warm up before a question session, or to fill a gap the Progress tab flagged.
